history_edu Our Heritage & Purpose

Making Wholesome, Plant-Based Ingredients Accessible & Consistent

For over 30 years, Seenergy Foods has been a family-owned business built around a simple idea: making wholesome, plant-based ingredients more accessible, convenient, and consistent.

Founded in 1994 by the Ajmera family, Seenergy began with a vision to bring high-quality beans and grains to a growing market that wanted healthier, convenient food options. Today, as a second-generation family business, that vision continues to shape everything we do.

We specialize in ready-to-eat, individually quick-frozen (IQF) beans, grains, lentils, pastas, and custom blends, supplying foodservice and food manufacturers across North America. Our products are designed to deliver the quality, consistency, and convenience today’s kitchens and food brands need.

“From our family to the millions of meals our products become every year, we’re proud to play a small part in making wholesome, plant-based food easier to serve every day.”
