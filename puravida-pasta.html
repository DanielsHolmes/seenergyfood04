IQF Penne Pasta

Tavola Classica • Rigati Durum Wheat

Penne pasta is one of Italy’s most popular shapes, easily recognized by its angled cut and ridged surface. Its design makes it perfect for holding thick sauces, from rich tomato blends to creamy alfredo or pesto. Made from durum wheat, penne provides lasting energy, is low in fat, and cholesterol-free.
